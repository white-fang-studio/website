Privacy Policy - Vellora: Signal Flow

Last updated: 11/25/2026

Vellora: Signal Flow ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android and iOS published by White Fang Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, what is shared with others, and the choices you have. We have no accounts, no servers of our own, and we do not sell personal information.

1. Summary

  • The game works fully offline. Your progress, settings and statistics are stored on your device.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or microphone.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob). Google may collect device and advertising identifiers for this. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states) you are asked for consent first.
  • You can remove interstitial ads with an in-app purchase. Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see your payment details.
  • Optional cloud save keeps a copy of your progress in your own Google Play Games or iCloud account.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device: levels completed and star ratings, settings (sound, music, language, accessibility options), hints balance, equipped and unlocked themes, achievements and statistics, daily and endless challenge progress, and the player names and results of the local two-player mode. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game removes it, unless you use cloud save (section 5).

3. Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show two kinds of ads: optional rewarded videos (watched in exchange for a hint) and interstitial ads between levels. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process, depending on your consent and settings:

  • advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or the Apple IDFA, where available and allowed) and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interaction and performance data, crash and diagnostic data.

We do not receive this data and cannot identify you from it. See Google's explanation: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • Where consent is required, the game shows Google's consent form at start. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Review ad privacy choices. Declining does not stop you from playing.
  • You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Buying Remove Ads removes interstitial ads. Rewarded videos stay optional.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases (Remove Ads and premium themes) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive only confirmation that a product was purchased. The game stores which products you own on your device so that they work offline. Restoring purchases uses your store account.

5. Cloud save (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games (Android) or are signed in to iCloud (iOS), the game stores a copy of your progress (levels, stars, settings, themes, achievements, statistics) in your own Google or Apple account so it can be restored on a new or second device. It is stored by Google or Apple, not by us, and is governed by their policies. Purchases are not stored in the cloud copy; they are restored through the store. You can delete the cloud copy through your Google Play Games or iCloud settings.

6. Network use

Besides ads, purchases and cloud save, the game makes a small request to a public web server (a Google or Apple test address) only to read the current date from the response, which protects the daily challenge reward from a manipulated device clock. No information about you or your game is sent in this request.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context advertising ourselves.
  • We do not run accounts, leaderboards or online multiplayer.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help. Ads are configured by the age settings required by the stores.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete. Data held by Google (advertising, Play Games) or Apple (iCloud, purchases) is managed through those companies. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent; you can exercise them with the company holding the data, and contact us with any question.

10. Data security and retention

Your save file is stored in the game's private storage on your device and is protected against casual editing. Local data is kept until you uninstall the game or choose Reset Progress in Settings (progress only) .

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes (for example when a new feature is added). The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be mentioned in the game's release notes.
